Frequently asked questions

QI have no confidence in my English. Is it OK for beginners?

Yes. The Beginner course starts with easy material and includes plenty of Japanese support early on. The placement test suggests the course that suits you.

QHow much time do I need each week?

There are two lessons a week, each split into parts of about 30 minutes, so you can do them on your commute or in spare moments. The daily micro submission takes one or two minutes.

QWhat do I need?

A smartphone or a computer is enough. You record your voice directly in the browser, so there is no app to install.

QIf I cancel during the free trial, will I be charged?

No. If you cancel during the 7-day free trial, no payment is taken.

QDo I have to join a bootcamp?

No, bootcamps are optional. You join the in-person Ryugaku lessons in Niseko or Nozawa Onsen. There is a separate fee, which varies by season.

QWhat happens when the 6-month course ends?

The course finishes after 6 months and does not renew automatically. If you want to continue, you can move on to the next level. No vocabulary is repeated between levels.
